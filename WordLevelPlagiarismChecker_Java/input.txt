Artificial intelligence is becoming an important technology in modern education. 
It can help students understand difficult concepts, receive personalized learning support, 
and complete educational tasks more efficiently. AI-based tools can also help teachers 
analyze student performance and identify areas where additional support is needed.

However, the use of artificial intelligence in education also creates challenges. 
Students may become overly dependent on automated tools, and incorrect information 
generated by AI can affect the quality of learning. Therefore, AI should be used 
carefully and responsibly as a support for human learning rather than as a complete 
replacement for teachers.
