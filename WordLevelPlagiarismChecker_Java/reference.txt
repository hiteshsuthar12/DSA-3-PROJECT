Artificial intelligence is playing an increasingly important role in modern education. 
It helps students understand complex topics, provides personalized learning assistance, 
and allows them to complete certain educational activities more efficiently. AI systems 
can also support teachers by analyzing student performance and finding areas where students 
need additional help.

Despite these advantages, artificial intelligence introduces several challenges in education.
 Students can become too dependent on automated systems, while inaccurate AI-generated 
 information may negatively influence learning outcomes. For this reason, AI should be 
 used responsibly as a learning aid and should not completely replace human teachers.
